## ⏱️ How to Use These Templates

1. **Pick your niche** from above
2. **Copy the exact values** into the prompts when you run the script
3. **Run:** `node influencer_scout_generalized.js`
4. When asked, **paste the values** from the template
5. **Let it run** — no tweaking needed!

---

## 🎯 Tips for Customizing

- **Want micro instead of nano?** Change Min/Max followers. E.g., for Beauty: 10K–100K
- **Different region?** Change hashtags to region-specific (e.g., #londonbeauty, #tokyofashion)
- **Want higher budget?** Raise follower range (macro = more expensive)
- **Too few results?** Lower min followers or add more hashtags
